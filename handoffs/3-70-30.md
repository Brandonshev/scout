## How it trades
**Copy trading (70% of the account):**
- Once a day it pulls Hyperliquid's public leaderboard and checks ~60 promising wallets on their **real trades from the last 30 days**:
  - profit after fees, including losses still sitting in open positions;
  - profitable in at least 2 of 3 ten-day stretches;
  - at least 10 closed trades;
  - profit factor ≥ 1.3 (money won ÷ money lost).
- It skips market-making bots (too fast to copy) and wallets with open losses over 10% of their account.
- It follows the top ~10 wallets, checking them every 20 seconds.
- When a followed wallet opens a **new** position, it copies it with 7% of the account. It can copy shorts too (fake money only).
- It closes the copy when the wallet closes. Positions the wallet already had are never copied, because by then it would be buying late.

**High-risk coins (30%):**
- Every 15 minutes it looks for small coins up ≥15% in 24 hours with a volume surge (3× normal).
- It needs a tight spread and at least US$1,000 of buyers within 5%.
- It buys 6% of the account, with fills simulated against the real order book.
- It sells at +50% or after 48 hours. Most are expected to lose.

**News check:**
- Five free RSS feeds are read every 10 minutes.
- A serious headline (hack, exploit, rug pull, delisting...) about a *smaller* coin blocks buying it, and sells it if held.
- Big coins (BTC, ETH, XRP...) are exempt, because they show up in hack stories as the stolen money.

**Limits:**
- no per-trade stop losses;
- at most 10% of the account per position;
- leverage 1x;
- a daily limit at −20%;
- a kill switch at −50% (last resort only).

## Known weaknesses to watch
- **Copying is always late:** we buy after the wallet, at a worse price. If copies lag their wallets badly, that's the cost showing.
- **Too-good wallets.** The current "best" wallet shows a 96% win rate. That's often a trader who holds losers instead of closing them. Open losses are checked at selection, but stay suspicious.
- **Sleep blind spot.** If Brandon's Mac sleeps, new positions opened meanwhile are deliberately not copied.
- **High-risk results swing on a few big wins.** Judge on totals over many trades, not win rate.
- **News matching is crude** (keywords). The scorecard tracks whether blocked or sold coins actually fell.
